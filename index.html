ℹ️ About Rent Easy

Rent Easy is a secure, user-friendly room rental marketplace designed for Zimbabwe. Our platform connects landlords and renters with a simple, transparent interface.

🎯 Our Mission

To make room rental in Zimbabwe easy, safe, and transparent for everyone.

✨ Key Features

  • 📱 Browse listings across major Zimbabwean cities
  • 🔐 Secure anonymous authentication
  • 💬 Direct WhatsApp contact with landlords
  • 🖼️ High-quality property images
  • 🏷️ Transparent pricing
  • 📝 Easy listing management

🌍 Coverage Areas

  • Harare - Capital city listings
  • Bulawayo - Second largest city
  • Mutare - Eastern region
  • Gweru - Central region
  • Masvingo - Southern region

💡 How It Works

  1. Create an anonymous account
  2. Browse available listings or post your own
  3. Contact landlords directly via WhatsApp
  4. Arrange viewings and finalize rentals

🤝 Support

Have questions? Contact us through the WhatsApp links on individual listings or reach out to our support team.

📜 Terms & Conditions

By using Rent Easy, you agree to conduct all transactions responsibly and verify property details before committing to a rental agreement.
